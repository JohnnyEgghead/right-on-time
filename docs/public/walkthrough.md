# Dispatcher walkthrough: 10 tasks

This is the walkthrough script for a new dispatcher. It has 10 tasks. Do them in order, with the training sheet beside you.

The goal is to finish all 10 tasks in 60 minutes, including the time you spend reading the training sheet. Write down your start time and your finish time.

All names, addresses and phone numbers in the app are made up. Nothing you do here reaches a real customer.

## How to read a step

- Words in quotes are exactly what the app shows on the screen. Find them and use them.
- "Click" means press a button, a link, a tab or a block.
- "Pick" means choose one item from a list of choices.
- "Type" means put the words in the box that has that name.
- "See" means look for those words on the screen. If you do not see them, stop and tell your trainer.
- Where a step says "your work email" or "your password", use the ones you were given.

Start with the app at the sign-in address you were given. The demo clock starts on Tue, Sep 15 at 7:00 AM.

## Task 1. Sign in

Screen: "Sign in"
Control: "Sign in"

You sign in to the office app with your own email and password. Your trainer makes this sign-in just before you start (see "Your sign-in" in the training sheet). A reset of the demo erases it. If the page says your sign-in and password do not match, ask your trainer to make it again.

1. Go to "/sign-in". This is the sign-in page.
2. See "Sign in".
3. Type your work email in "Work email".
4. Type your password in "Password".
5. Click "Sign in". The dispatch board opens.
6. See "Jobs today".

## Task 2. Read the board

Screen: "Jobs today"
Control: "Needs attention"

The board shows every technician's day. The numbers at the top tell you what needs you first.

1. See "Jobs today". This is how many visits are on the day.
2. See "Unassigned". Visits with no technician wait here.
3. See "Technicians". The rows below it are the technicians on shift.
4. Click "Needs attention". This tab lists the late, at risk and unassigned visits.
5. See "At risk". A visit that may run late is marked with these words.

## Task 3. Open a job

Screen: "Job 24830"
Control: "Open job"

Every visit on the board is a block. A click on the block shows a small card. The card leads to the whole job.

1. Click "Harold Jenkins". This is a block on the board. A block can cut long words short. Click the block to see all its words.
2. See "Job 24830". The card shows the job number.
3. Click "Open job". The job page opens.
4. See "Job 24830".
5. See "Furnace tune-up - Harold Jenkins".

## Task 4. Move a job to another technician

Screen: "Job 24830"
Control: "Reassign"

You stay on the job page from Task 3. Here you give the visit to a different technician.

1. See "Job 24830". You are on the job page.
2. Click "Reassign". A list of technicians opens.
3. See "Reassign job 24830".
4. Pick "Ryan Mitchell".
5. Click "Reassign". This button is inside the list.
6. See "Reassigned by". The job history now says who moved the visit.

## Task 5. Add a note to a job

Screen: "Job 24830"
Control: "Add note"

Notes help the next person who opens the job. A note cannot be changed or deleted after you save it.

1. See "Job 24830". You are on the job page.
2. Click "Add note".
3. Type "Gate code is 4471." in "Note".
4. Click "Save note".
5. See "Note added".
6. See "Gate code is 4471.".

## Task 6. Text the customer

Screen: "Job 24830"
Control: "Text customer"

You can send the customer a text from the job page. No text goes out between 9 PM and 8 AM the customer's time. The demo clock starts at 7:00 AM (see the start of this guide), so while it reads before 8:00 AM the text waits until 8:00 AM.

1. See "Job 24830". You are on the job page.
2. Click "Text customer".
3. Type "We will arrive between 10 AM and 12 PM." in "Message".
4. Click "Send".
5. See "Office text sent".

## Task 7. Reschedule a job

Screen: "Job 24830"
Control: "Reschedule"

The customer asks for another day. You move the visit to a day and an arrival window that have room.

1. See "Job 24830". You are on the job page.
2. Click "Reschedule". A list of days opens.
3. Pick "Thu, Sep 17". A full day says "Full" and cannot be picked.
4. Pick "10 AM-12 PM". This is the arrival window.
5. Click "Reschedule". This button is inside the list.
6. See "Thu, Sep 17, 2026".

## Task 8. Add a time block

Screen: "Jobs today"
Control: "Add block"

A time block holds a technician's time for something that is not a job, such as a supply run. It shows on the technician's row.

1. Click "Board". This is the link at the top of every page.
2. See "Jobs today".
3. Click "Add block".
4. Choose "Maria Santos" in "Technician".
5. Type "Supply run" in "Label".
6. Type "Parts counter" in "Place".
7. Click the first number in "Start". This is the hour. Do not click the second number: typing there leaves the box empty.
8. Type "01:00:P" in "Start". This is 1:00 PM. Each colon moves you to the next part of the box. If the box shows 24-hour times, type 13:00 instead.
9. Click the first number in "End". This is the hour.
10. Type "01:30:P" in "End". This is 1:30 PM. If the box shows 24-hour times, type 13:30 instead.
11. Click "Add block". This button is inside the form. It stays gray until both times are in the boxes.
12. See "Supply run". The block on the row can cut long words short. Click the block to see all its words.

## Task 9. Book a new job

Screen: "New job"
Control: "Schedule"

A customer calls for a visit. You find the customer, pick the service, pick the day and window, and pick who goes.

1. Click "New job".
2. See "New job".
3. Type "Jenkins" in "Customer".
4. Click "Harold Jenkins". The customer is picked.
5. Pick "Furnace tune-up". This is the service.
6. Pick "Mon, Sep 21". This is the day.
7. Pick "10 AM-12 PM". This is the arrival window.
8. See "Suggested technicians".
9. Click "Pick" beside "Maria Santos". The list is ranked by skill, distance and load.
10. Click "Schedule".
11. See "Booked by the office".

## Task 10. Cancel a visit

Screen: "Cancel visit"
Control: "Cancel the visit"

The customer cancels. You cancel the visit and the job stays in the history. No text is sent to the customer.

1. Click "Board".
2. See "Jobs today".
3. Click "Debra Desai". This is a block on the board. Click the block to see all its words.
4. Click "Open job".
5. Click "Cancel visit". A question opens.
6. See "Cancel visit".
7. See "No text is sent to the customer."
8. Click "Cancel the visit". This is the red button.
9. See "Canceled".
