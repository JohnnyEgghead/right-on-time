# Dispatcher training sheet

This sheet is for a new dispatcher. Read it once, then do the 10 tasks of the walkthrough (walkthrough.md). Plan on 60 minutes for both.

## What you do all day

You make sure every visit has a technician and an arrival window, and that every customer knows what to expect. The board shows you where each visit stands. You step in when something is late, at risk or unassigned.

## Before you start

1. Get the address of the app, your work email and your password from your trainer. Your trainer makes your sign-in just before you start (see "Your sign-in" below).
2. Open the address in a web browser on a computer.
3. Know that the app uses made-up data. Nothing you do reaches a real customer.
4. Write down your start time.

## Your sign-in

Your trainer makes your office sign-in on the demo, as a manager. Every reset of the demo erases it, so your trainer makes it after the last reset and before you start. If the sign-in page says your sign-in and password do not match, a reset may have erased your account. Ask your trainer to make it again.

Trainer steps, in the office app signed in as a manager:

1. Click "Admin", then click "Demo clock". Under "Reset the demo world", click the button that starts "Reset to". In the question "Reset the demo world?", click "Reset the world".
2. Click "Admin", then click "Users and roles". Click "Invite user".
3. Type the person's name in "Name" and their email in "Email". Pick "Manager". Pick the branch.
4. Click "Send invite". Click "Open the invite email". The email is in the on-screen inbox. Nothing is really sent.
5. Click "Set your password". Type a password of 10 or more characters in "New password". Click "Save password".
6. Give the person the address of the app, the email and the password.

## The screens you use

- **Sign in.** The page where you enter your work email and password. Press "Sign in".
- **Board.** The first screen after you sign in. Each technician has a row. Each visit is a block on the row. Open it from the "Board" link at the top of any page.
- **The job page.** It opens from a block on the board, with "Open job". It shows the customer, the equipment, the notes and what happened on the visit.
- **New job.** It opens from the "New job" button on the board. You book a visit here.

## Words on the board

- **Jobs today.** The number of visits on the day.
- **Late.** The technician's estimated arrival is after the end of the arrival window. A visit can show Late before the window ends. The customer's late text has its own rule: it goes out when the estimate is 20 or more minutes later than the time the customer was last told.
- **At risk.** The visit may run late.
- **Unassigned.** The visit has no technician yet. It waits in the Unassigned row.
- **Needs attention.** A tab that lists the late, at risk and unassigned visits. Look here first.
- **Simulated pilot data.** The numbers come from made-up data in this training copy.

## The 10 tasks

You do these in order. Each one names its screen and the control it uses.

1. Sign in
2. Read the board
3. Open a job
4. Move a job to another technician
5. Add a note to a job
6. Text the customer
7. Reschedule a job
8. Add a time block
9. Book a new job
10. Cancel a visit

## Good habits

- Check "Needs attention" before you do anything else.
- Read the job page before you text or call the customer.
- Pick an arrival window that shows room. A window marked "Full" cannot be picked.
- Add a note when you learn something the next person needs. A note cannot be changed or deleted after you save it.
- No text goes out between 9 PM and 8 AM the customer's time, with 2 exceptions: the on the way text and the running late text go out at any hour. Every other text sent then waits until 8 AM.
- If a screen does not look like this sheet, stop and ask your trainer. Do not guess.

## When you are done

Write down your finish time. Tell your trainer how long it took and which step, if any, was hard to follow.
