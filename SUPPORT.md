# Support: Right On Time

Right On Time is a free, open-source project. Support is through the public Issues page:

https://github.com/JohnnyEgghead/right-on-time/issues

Open an issue to report a bug, ask a question or suggest a change. A good report says:

1. What you did, step by step.
2. What you expected and what happened.
3. The iPhone model and the iOS version.

Please do not put passwords, real personal information or private photos in an issue. Everything on the public sample site is made-up sample data, and the site resets every night.

The privacy policy is in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
